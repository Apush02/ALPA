FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you offer refunds?+

We know life happens. But to protect the quality of our small-group experience, we don't offer refunds. What we will do is roll your registration over to the next available session or issue a credit toward any of our other programs — just let us know what works best for you.

Is ALPA's content accredited?+

Our focus isn't paperwork — it's results. Every program is built around high-yield topics and real exam patterns, shaped by instructors who know exactly what shows up on test day. We'd rather give you test-relevant knowledge than a certificate that doesn't move the needle.

Can I try before I commit?+

Yes. We offer a free sample lesson so you can see exactly how we teach before you enroll — no guessing, no risk.

Why not just study alone with free resources online?+

You absolutely can, if you have unlimited time to sort through what's relevant and what isn't. Most students don't. Beyond saving you those hundreds of hours, ALPA gives you what free resources can't: structured practice through Anki and MCQs built around real exam patterns, and a supportive community around you instead of studying alone in silence.

What if the group schedule doesn't fit my week?+

We run our small groups on a consistent weekly schedule to suit diverse student availability and keep the learning experience effective. Not sure if the timing suits you? Contact us before enrolling, and we'll help match you to the right group.

How much time do I need to commit each week?+

We recommend committing roughly 2–3 hours per day. This allows you to cover the daily material, review thoroughly, and still have time to enjoy life without feeling overwhelmed. Consistency is more important than cramming, so this pace is designed to be sustainable. Fridays are always off for you to rest 😉
